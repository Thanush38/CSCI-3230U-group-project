Plank

Home Workout Planner

Muscle Group: Waist

Target Muscles: Abs

Equipment: Bodyweight

Intructions

Step:1 Start in a push-up position with your hands shoulder-width apart and your body straight.

Step:2 Engage your core and slowly shift your weight forward, bringing your shoulders past your hands.

Step:3 Keep your elbows slightly bent and your body straight as you lean forward.

Step:4 Hold this position for as long as you can.

Step:5 Repeat for the desired number of repetitions.
